About the Project

This platform aims to support the kingdom's economic independence while serving as a valuable tool for the people.

Revenue Streams

  • Tiered Corporate Access: International partners and investors pay a subscription or licensing fee to access the Economic Development Portal for verified business opportunities and kingdom-sanctioned projects.
  • Royal Marketplace: A curated e-commerce platform that takes a commission on high-end, authentic kingdom exports—such as luxury timepieces, artisanal fashion, and traditional crafts—shipped globally.
  • The "Digital Citizenship" Fee: A nominal annual fee for subjects to maintain their status in the Royal Registry, providing them with official digital ID and priority access to kingdom events.
  • Cultural Content Subscriptions: Premium access for researchers, historians, or the diaspora to the Oral History Vault and specialized language-learning modules.
  • Summit & Event Management: Charging processing or registration fees for corporate delegates attending international summits or royal business forums coordinated through the app.
  • Government & NGO Grants: Positioning the app as a "Cultural Preservation Tool" to secure developmental funding for digitizing heritage and community infrastructure.
